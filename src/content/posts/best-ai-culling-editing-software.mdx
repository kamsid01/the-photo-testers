## First, know what you actually need

"AI photo software" covers three distinct post-production jobs, and most tools specialize in one:

- **Culling** is selecting keepers from thousands of raw frames, flagging blinks, soft focus, and duplicate expressions (Aftershoot, Narrative Select, FilterPixel).
- **Editing** is applying your color grading, exposure balance, and tone curves across the entire shoot (Aftershoot Edit, Imagen AI).
- **Retouching** is the detailed per-subject work: skin smoothing, blemish cleanup, glare reduction, stray hair removal, and backdrop cleanups (Evoto, Aftershoot Retouch).

Buy for the specific task that is actually eating your time.

## 1. Aftershoot: best all-in-one

Aftershoot does the whole post-shoot workflow in one offline app: culling, editing, and retouching. It runs on your own machine (so your files never leave your computer), and the pricing is flat and unlimited rather than per-image, which is the key reason high-volume photographers pick it.

Aftershoot pricing uses three modular flat-fee plans: Select (culling only) at $120/year ($10/month billed annually) or $15/month monthly; Edit (AI editing with personalized or marketplace profiles) at $360/year ($30/month billed annually) or $25/month monthly; and Complete (bundling culling, editing, and portrait retouching) at $540/year ($40/month billed annually) or $49/month monthly. Processing is 100% on-device on Apple Silicon or Windows GPUs, so your image volume never increases your bill.

The editing learns your style, but there's a real requirement to know: Personal AI Profiles learn your specific editing style from 2,500+ of your previously edited RAW images (5,000+ recommended for best accuracy). So you need a back catalogue of your own edits for the personal profile to shine. If you don't have that yet, you can start with the marketplace styles built by other photographers.

The honest limitation: AI accuracy varies by shooting conditions: low-light and high-action scenes require more manual override. So it's not fully hands-off in tricky light, you'll still give the cull a second look.

## 2. Imagen AI: best editing quality

Imagen is the tool photographers reach for when editing quality inside Lightroom matters most. It's cloud-based, learns your style through a personal profile, and is well known for nailing skin tones on weddings and portraits. It also offers a library of "Talent" profiles built by top-tier photographers.

Imagen offers two primary billing models: Pay-as-you-go (PAYG) and a flat Limitless subscription. On PAYG, base editing costs $0.05 per photo (subject to a $7 monthly minimum spend). For eligible solo photographers shooting their own work, Imagen also offers a flat Limitless plan ($129/month billed annually ($1,548/year) or $165/month billed monthly) covering unlimited AI editing with a personal profile and AI tools included. Studios with associate shooters or teams use pooled Business plans.

On PAYG, optional AI tools add $0.01 per photo each: Crop ($0.01), Straighten ($0.01), Subject Mask ($0.01), and Smooth Skin ($0.01). With all four add-ons enabled, editing rises from $0.05 to $0.09 per photo—an 80% increase over base editing. Because pricing applies to delivered, culled edits (e.g. 700 frames from a wedding), PAYG is highly cost-effective for lower-volume portrait photographers, while solo wedding shooters benefit from Limitless.

Two more honest limits: it's cloud-only (so no offline editing, and you need fast internet), and it works with Lightroom Classic only; Capture One and Lightroom Desktop aren't supported. Its culling exists but is a secondary feature; don't buy Imagen primarily to cull.

## 3. Narrative Select: best culling control

If you want the AI to help you cull faster while keeping full creative decision-making in your hands, Narrative Select is the premier choice. It offers exceptionally fast zero-lag RAW preview browsing and automatically assesses subject focus and closed eyes with red and yellow warning badges.

Narrative operates 100% locally and offline on your computer. Plan availability has transitioned: official documentation outlines Lite, Standard, Pro/Premium, and Ultra subscription tiers (starting from approximately $8 to $18/month billed annually), while older marketing pages still advertise a legacy free tier. A free trial is available to evaluate the software.

The trade-off versus Aftershoot: Narrative assists your culling rather than fully automating it, so it's less "drop it and walk away" and more "review at speed." Whether that's a pro or con depends on how much you want the AI deciding for you.

## 4. FilterPixel: best culling on a budget

FilterPixel is the value pick for culling. Its big draw is a genuinely free way to evaluate it: you can try FilterPixel on 4 projects for free, including their DeepCull feature, with no credit card required. Paid plans are competitive, and its cloud processing offloads computational heavy lifting from older laptops.

Its headline feature is genre-aware culling: "Deep Cull" is a genre-aware form of culling that not only culls for sharpness and blinks, but also looks for emotions and facial expressions. That makes it useful for deadline shooters, sports, events, and same-day weddings, where the AI needs to understand what makes a good shot in that specific context.

## 5. Evoto: best portrait retouching

Evoto is the retouching specialist. For portrait, headshot, wedding, and boudoir photographers, it handles skin smoothing, blemish removal, glasses-glare removal, stray hairs, and background work, mostly with sliders, and applies edits across a batch. Photographers consistently praise how much outsourced retouching it replaces. It added AI culling in late 2025, but retouching is its core.

The pricing is different from the subscription tools: editing and previewing edited images is free. Exporting an image requires an Evoto credit. Credits can be purchased as a single package with a 2 year expiration date, or as an annual subscription. A reviewer's honest take: it's fantastic for retouching but its credit-per-image payment system requires tracking edits and credits so you don't exceed your budget.

One thing to be aware of: Evoto faced user feedback earlier over an AI headshot generator feature. The company removed the content and stated it does not train its AI on user images. Worth knowing, though it's a retouching tool at heart and the core editing is well liked.

## The honest truth about all of them

Two things worth saying plainly.

**No AI tool replaces your judgment.** The realistic gain is time, not a hands-off pipeline. Across the tools, photographers report cutting post-production by 50 to 70 percent: a 2,500-image wedding that took an entire evening drops to under 30 minutes of active review. That's a huge saving. But you still review the cull and check the edits.

**Most pros use more than one.** A common stack is one tool for culling (e.g. Narrative Select or Aftershoot), another for bulk color editing (Imagen), and a specialist for retouching hero frames (Evoto).

## FAQ

**What is the best AI culling software for photographers?**
For fully automated culling at a flat price, Aftershoot. For fast culling where you keep creative control, Narrative Select. For budget or deadline work, FilterPixel's genre-aware DeepCull. The best fit depends on how much you want the AI to decide.

**Is Aftershoot or Imagen better?**
Aftershoot is the better all-in-one and better value at high volume thanks to flat workstation pricing. Imagen has excellent editing quality in Lightroom Classic with flexible PAYG and a solo Limitless plan. Many photographers cull in Aftershoot and edit in Imagen.

**How much time does AI culling actually save?**
Photographers commonly report cutting post-production time by 50 to 70 percent. A wedding cull that took a full evening can drop to under 30 minutes of active review.

**Do these tools work offline?**
Aftershoot, Narrative Select, and Evoto work offline on local hardware. Imagen AI and FilterPixel require an internet connection for cloud processing.

**What's the best AI tool for portrait retouching?**
Evoto is the leading portrait retouching tool for skin, glare, and background work. Aftershoot also includes retouching in its Complete bundle.
