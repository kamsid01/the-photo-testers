## What Fylm.ai is, and why photographers use it

[Fylm.ai](https://fylm.ai/) is a browser-based colour grading platform built on ACEScct colour science. It allows creators to grade stills and motion footage in the cloud without installing heavy desktop software, combining colour-management wheels with AI features like AI Color Extract and its NeuralFilmAI engine.

While popular with cinematographers, Fylm.ai attracts photographers because it bridges the gap between cinematic grading and stills editing. Photographers can upload RAW or TIFF files, develop a bespoke film grade, and export the result as standard 3D LUTs (.CUBE), enhanced XMP camera profiles for Adobe Lightroom Classic and Camera Raw, or ICC profiles for Capture One Pro. This allows you to design a look in the cloud and apply it non-destructively to thousands of images in your desktop catalog.

According to [Fylm.ai's official pricing](https://fylm.ai/pricing/) (verified October 2026), the platform offers four subscription tiers:

- **Free ($0)**: 3 projects, 1 GB cloud storage, standard 8-bit web grading tools, and community support.
- **Lite ($7/month billed annually / $84/year, or $9/month billed monthly)**: 10 projects, 10 GB cloud storage, 3D LUT and XMP/ICC profile export.
- **Pro ($15/month billed annually / $180/year, or $19/month billed monthly)**: Unlimited projects, 50 GB cloud storage, NeuralFilmAI, AI Color Extract, RAW image support, and 33-point / 65-point 3D LUT exports.
- **Team ($30/month billed annually / $360/year, or $39/month billed monthly)**: Multi-seat team collaboration, 100 GB storage, and priority rendering.

## Why look for a Fylm.ai alternative

While Fylm.ai provides an elegant browser interface, photographers frequently look for alternatives due to three key workflow factors:

**1. Browser and cloud round-tripping vs. in-editor plugins.** Fylm.ai requires uploading your photos to a browser, grading them in the cloud, and downloading LUTs or profiles back to your machine. Many portrait, commercial, and wedding photographers prefer desktop plugins that operate directly within Lightroom Classic, Photoshop, or Capture One without round-tripping intermediate files.

**2. Video-first design and unnecessary overhead.** Fylm.ai, ACES workflows, and similar cinematic suites are engineered with video productions in mind. If you strictly shoot still photography, much of the timeline and motion color science adds unnecessary operational complexity.

**3. Ongoing subscription fees vs. perpetual ownership.** Fylm.ai is a recurring cloud SaaS subscription. For creators who prefer buying software once and owning it perpetually, dedicated desktop profile generators like 3D LUT Creator or Dehancer's lifetime options provide substantial long-term value.

### Dehancer Photo (Film emulation inside your editor)

![Dehancer Photo interface showing film stock profiles and controls](/assets/dehancer-photo.webp)

If you use Fylm.ai primarily to achieve realistic analogue film tones, [Dehancer Photo](https://www.dehancer.com/shop/dehancer-photo) is the strongest direct desktop alternative. Rather than operating in a separate cloud environment, Dehancer runs natively as a plugin inside Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Lightroom Classic, Capture One (via external editor workflow), and Affinity Photo.

Dehancer's core strength is its optical physics engine. It models film as a multi-stage analogue process rather than applying simple 1D or 3D color curves. It includes over 60 carefully measured film stock profiles (Kodak Portra, Ektar, Tri-X, Fujifilm Velvia, Provia, Ilford HP5, CineStill 800T), authentic optical film grain sampled from physical emulsions, halation (the reddish glow that appears around high-contrast edges on motion picture film), bloom (diffuse highlight glow), and print paper profiles (such as Kodak Endura and 2383).

**System requirements and limitations:**
- **OS:** macOS 12+ (macOS 15 Sequoia recommended, Metal GPU required) and Windows 10/11 64-bit (Nvidia or AMD OpenCL GPU required).
- **Workflow:** Dehancer operates on rasterized data or high-bitdepth TIFFs sent from Lightroom or Capture One. It supports batch editing using its "Last Edits" memory and Photoshop Batch Actions.
- **Pricing:** Available as a subscription for $13/month or $156/year, or as a perpetual lifetime license from $199 (single host) to $449 (multi-host bundle). A free watermarked trial is available from [Dehancer's download center](https://www.dehancer.com/).

### Colourlab Ai 4 (AI grading for hybrid shooter timelines)

[Colourlab Ai 4](https://colourlab.ai/products/colourlab-ai-4/) is an advanced AI-powered grading application designed to automate color matching and look creation. Unlike Fylm.ai's browser model, Colourlab Ai 4 is a dedicated desktop application engineered specifically for video timelines and hybrid creators.

It is critical to note Colourlab Ai 4's exact architecture: it is **not a photo-editor plugin** for Lightroom or Photoshop. Instead, it connects to professional video editing suites:
- **DaVinci Resolve Studio:** Direct two-way Fetch and Push API connection.
- **DaVinci Resolve Free:** OpenTimelineIO (OTIO) file roundtripping.
- **Adobe Premiere Pro:** OTIO timeline roundtripping (a direct link integration is currently planned).
- **Apple Final Cut Pro:** XML roundtripping (direct link integration planned).

**System requirements and limitations:**
- **Hardware & OS:** Strictly requires Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3, or M4 chips) and macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. Intel Macs and Windows PCs are not supported.
- **Still photography:** Native RAW still photo support is currently listed by Colourlab as forthcoming. Photographers who shoot hybrid video projects will benefit most, while stills-only photographers will find it overpowered and cumbersome for catalog workflows.
- **Pricing:** $49/month, $399/year annual subscription, or $799 perpetual lifetime license, backed by a 14-day free trial.

### 3D LUT Creator (Surgical color grading and profile generation)

![3D LUT Creator interface showing A/B color grid grading](/assets/3d-lut-creator.webp)

If your goal with Fylm.ai is creating custom 3D LUTs and ICC profiles with absolute color precision, [3D LUT Creator](https://3dlutcreator.com/) is the undisputed industry benchmark. Developed by Oleg Sharonov, it is a standalone desktop application for macOS and Windows with a direct two-way plugin bridge to Adobe Photoshop.

Unlike traditional grading wheels that adjust broad tonal ranges, 3D LUT Creator uses a revolutionary 2D A/B color grid and C/L contrast grid. You can select any precise hue and saturation point (for instance, shifting skin tones away from magenta or twisting green foliage into warm amber) and drag the grid vertex directly without shifting neighboring colors or introducing digital banding.

**Key capabilities for photographers:**
- **Profile exports:** Exports universal 3D LUTs (.CUBE, 3DL), curves, Adobe Lightroom / Camera Raw enhanced profiles (.xmp), and Capture One ICC camera profiles (.icc).
- **Photoshop integration:** Fetch and send images back and forth between Photoshop and 3D LUT Creator with a single click.
- **Pricing:** No recurring monthly subscriptions. It is sold under a perpetual license:
  - **Grading Edition ($99):** Core A/B grid, RGB curves, and 3D LUT export.
  - **Standard Edition ($199):** Adds 2D curves, channel mixer, and Photoshop integration.
  - **Pro Edition ($249):** Unlocks full ICC profile generation for Capture One, ACES color space management, and advanced masking.

### Polarr (One-click reference matching for photo batches)

[Polarr](https://www.polarr.com/) (and its modern web-first platform Polarr Next) is the most photographer-centric tool for rapid color matching. Rather than requiring you to understand ACES color management or build LUT curves by hand, Polarr uses AI Color Match to analyze any reference photo and transfer its palette, contrast, and tone to your own images.

**Photography workflow:**
If you see an editorial photograph, cinematic film still, or Pinterest board and want your shoot to match that aesthetic, you upload the reference image alongside your photos. Polarr aligns the exposures and color balances in one click.

**Pricing and access:**
Polarr offers a free tier with 2,000 initial processing credits. Paid usage operates on either pay-as-you-go pricing (roughly $0.05 per processed image) or lightweight recurring plans starting from $1.99 to $3.99/month, making it significantly cheaper than high-end grading platforms.

### Media.io (Free online auto-color correction)

[Media.io Auto Color Correct](https://www.media.io/auto-color-correct.html) is a lightweight, web-based utility for photographers and casual content creators who just need a quick, automated image fix.

Unlike Fylm.ai, Media.io is not a creative color grading or LUT creation suite. It uses automated algorithms to balance underexposed shadows, remove color casts, and correct white balance in a single click. It is completely browser-based, requires no installation, and is free for occasional use, though it lacks RAW file support and professional profile exports.

### Discontinued: Color.io (Shut down December 2025)

[Color.io](https://www.color.io/) was previously the most direct web-based alternative to Fylm.ai, offering film emulation and LUT creation in a browser.

**Important status update:** As reported by [CineD](https://www.cined.com/color-io-shutting-down-popular-film-emulation-and-color-grading-tool-goes-offline-december-31/), Color.io's founder officially announced that the online platform shut down on **31 December 2025**. While existing Pro subscribers received access to an offline legacy desktop build, the cloud platform is offline and closed to new registrations. We no longer recommend Color.io as an active alternative for photographers.

### Lightroom Classic and Capture One Pro (The tools you already own)

Before investing in a third-party color grading suite, it is worth evaluating the tools already inside your primary RAW converter:

- **Adobe Lightroom Classic:** Features 3-Way Color Grading wheels (Shadows, Midtones, Highlights, and Global), Point Color for surgical hue-saturation-luminance sampling, the Calibration panel (a favorite among portrait photographers for rich skin tones), and tone curves.
- **Capture One Pro:** Includes an industry-standard Advanced Color Editor with dedicated Skin Tone uniformity sliders, 3-Way Color Balance wheels, and per-layer opacity grading.

If your objective is simply clean, consistent, flattering color across weddings or portraits, mastering your RAW editor's built-in toolset often eliminates the need for external grading software.

## An honest word on fit

Most dedicated grading suites—Fylm.ai included—originate from the film and motion picture industry. They are invaluable for photographers who want film-stock physics, custom LUT creation, and profiles they can share across video and still projects.

However, workflow friction is real. Cloud roundtripping requires uploading heavy files, while standalone grading apps require exporting TIFF intermediates. Align your choice with your actual bottleneck:

- **For authentic film grain, halation, and stock profiles in your editor:** Choose [Dehancer Photo](https://www.dehancer.com/shop/dehancer-photo).
- **For surgical color warping and perpetual-license LUT/ICC export:** Choose [3D LUT Creator](https://3dlutcreator.com/).
- **For AI shot-matching across video timelines on Apple silicon:** Choose [Colourlab Ai 4](https://colourlab.ai/products/colourlab-ai-4/).
- **For quick, automated reference matching on photos:** Choose [Polarr](https://www.polarr.com/).
- **For general catalog color consistency:** Stick with your existing RAW editor.

## FAQ

**What is Fylm.ai used for?**
Fylm.ai is a cloud-based colour grading platform that uses ACEScct colour management to build cinematic looks and 3D LUTs. Photographers use it because it can export enhanced XMP camera profiles for Adobe Lightroom and Camera Raw, as well as ICC profiles for Capture One Pro.

**How much does Fylm.ai cost?**
Fylm.ai offers four plans: a Free tier (3 projects, 1 GB storage), Lite ($7/month billed annually or $9/month monthly), Pro ($15/month billed annually or $19/month monthly, unlocking NeuralFilmAI and RAW support), and Team ($30/month billed annually or $39/month monthly).

**What is the closest alternative to Fylm.ai for photographers?**
For film emulation and profiles inside your editing software, Dehancer Photo is the closest alternative. For precision color grading, custom 3D LUTs, and ICC profile creation with one-time perpetual licensing, 3D LUT Creator is the most versatile choice.

**What happened to Color.io?**
Color.io announced the shutdown of its online platform on 31 December 2025. While existing Pro subscribers received an offline legacy build, the service is discontinued and closed to new users.

**Does Colourlab Ai 4 work inside Lightroom or Photoshop?**
No. Colourlab Ai 4 is a standalone macOS application that requires Apple silicon (M-series) and macOS 14+. It connects directly with DaVinci Resolve Studio via API, and with Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro via OTIO/XML. Native RAW still photo support is currently listed as forthcoming.

**Is there a free alternative to Fylm.ai?**
Yes. Fylm.ai itself offers a free tier with 3 projects and 1 GB storage. Media.io offers free one-click auto colour correction in your browser. For photographers, your existing RAW editor (Lightroom Classic or Capture One) provides comprehensive color grading tools at no extra cost.

**Do photographers actually need a dedicated colour grading tool?**
Not always. If you need authentic film emulsion physics (like grain, halation, and bloom), multi-camera video matching, or custom LUT/ICC profile authoring, dedicated tools are invaluable. If your goal is clean, consistent portrait or event colour, your RAW editor's built-in tools are usually more than sufficient.
