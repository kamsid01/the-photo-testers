## What Fylm.ai is, and why photographers use it

[Fylm.ai](https://fylm.ai/) is a browser-based colour grading platform built on ACEScct colour science. It allows creators to grade stills and motion footage without installing heavy desktop suites, combining colour-management wheels with AI tools like AI Colour Extract and its NeuralFilmAI engine.

While widely used by cinematographers, Fylm.ai attracts photographers because it bridges the gap between cinematic grading and stills editing. Photographers can import RAW or TIFF files, develop a bespoke film grade, and export the result as standard 3D LUTs (.cube), enhanced XMP camera profiles for Adobe Lightroom Classic and Camera Raw, or ICC profiles for Capture One Pro. This allows you to design a look once and apply it non-destructively across an entire catalog.

Storage handling depends on the plan you choose. Fylm.ai's entry tier processes and stores assets locally on your own machine without mandatory cloud uploads, whereas its upper tiers use cloud storage to enable multi-user reviews and client sharing.

According to [Fylm.ai's official pricing](https://fylm.ai/pricing/) (verified November 2026), the platform offers four subscription tiers (billed annually):

- **Free ($0)**: Single user, 3 cloud projects, standard 8-bit web grading tools, and community support.
- **Lite ($7/month billed annually / $84/year)**: Single user with unlimited local storage and unlimited LUT creation. Includes NeuralFilmAI, AI Auto Correct, AI Colour Extract, and AI Colour Match.
- **Pro ($15/month billed annually / $180/year)**: Starts at 1 user (collaborators up to 5 users cost $15/user/month). Includes everything in Lite plus the Premiere Pro extension, NeuralToneAI, 10 GB cloud storage, client sharing, and client download permissions.
- **Team ($30/month billed annually / $360/year)**: Starts with 3 included users (collaborators up to 10 users cost $15/user/month). Includes everything in Pro plus 25 GB cloud storage, team-only comments, client-assignable statuses, and priority support.

## Why look for a Fylm.ai alternative

While Fylm.ai provides an intuitive interface, photographers frequently seek alternatives due to three key workflow factors:

**1. Browser workflows vs. in-editor plugins.** Even though Fylm.ai Lite uses local storage, you still work inside a web browser rather than directly inside your primary editing application. Many portrait, commercial, and wedding photographers prefer desktop plugins that operate directly within Lightroom Classic, Photoshop, or Capture One without switching environments.

**2. Video-first design and unnecessary overhead.** Fylm.ai, ACES pipelines, and similar cinematic suites are engineered with video productions in mind. If you strictly shoot still photography, much of the motion color science and timeline management adds operational complexity you may never use.

**3. Ongoing subscription fees vs. perpetual ownership.** Fylm.ai requires an annual subscription commitment. Creators who prefer buying software once and owning it permanently often turn to standalone profile generators like 3D LUT Creator or Dehancer's lifetime options.

Here is what to consider instead, and who each tool suits.

### Dehancer Photo (Film emulation inside your editor)

![Dehancer Photo promotional product display showing film stock presets and plugin controls](/assets/dehancer-photo.webp)

If you use Fylm.ai primarily to achieve realistic analogue film tones, [Dehancer Photo](https://www.dehancer.com/shop/dehancer-photo) is the strongest direct desktop alternative. Rather than operating in a separate browser window, Dehancer runs natively as a plugin inside Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Lightroom Classic, Capture One (via external editor workflow), and Affinity Photo.

Dehancer's core strength is its optical physics engine. It models film as a multi-stage analogue process rather than applying simple 1D or 3D color curves. It includes over 60 carefully measured film stock profiles (Kodak Portra, Ektar, Tri-X, Fujifilm Velvia, Provia, Ilford HP5, CineStill 800T), authentic optical film grain sampled from physical emulsions, halation (the reddish glow that appears around high-contrast edges on motion picture film), bloom (diffuse highlight glow), and print paper profiles (such as Kodak Endura and 2383).

**System requirements and workflow:**
- **OS:** macOS 12+ (macOS 15 Sequoia recommended, Metal GPU required) and Windows 10/11 64-bit (Nvidia or AMD OpenCL GPU required).
- **Workflow:** Dehancer operates on rasterized data or high-bitdepth TIFFs sent from Lightroom or Capture One. It supports batch editing using its "Last Edits" memory and Photoshop Batch Actions.
- **Pricing:** The official [Dehancer Photo Plan](https://www.dehancer.com/pricing/individual) is priced at **$156 billed annually** (equivalent to **$13/month**), or **$35/month** when paying monthly (includes 3 seats, 1 seat per app/host across desktop, iOS, and online). Photographers seeking permanent ownership can purchase an [Individual Lifetime License](https://www.dehancer.com/pricing/lifetime/pslr) for **$549** (includes 2 seats, 1 seat per host). A watermarked demo is available from [Dehancer's download center](https://www.dehancer.com/).

### Colourlab Ai 4 (AI grading for hybrid shooter timelines)

[Colourlab Ai 4](https://colourlab.ai/products/colourlab-ai-4/) is an advanced AI-powered grading application designed to automate color matching and look creation. Unlike Fylm.ai's browser model, Colourlab Ai 4 is a dedicated desktop application engineered specifically for video timelines and hybrid creators.

It is critical to note Colourlab Ai 4's exact architecture: it is **not a photo-editor plugin** for Lightroom or Photoshop. Instead, it connects to professional video editing suites:
- **DaVinci Resolve Studio:** Direct two-way Fetch and Push API connection.
- **DaVinci Resolve Free:** OpenTimelineIO (OTIO) file roundtripping.
- **Adobe Premiere Pro:** OTIO timeline roundtripping (a direct link integration is currently planned).
- **Apple Final Cut Pro:** XML roundtripping (direct link integration planned).

**System requirements and limitations:**
- **Hardware & OS:** Strictly requires Apple silicon (M1, M2, M3, or M4 chips) and macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. Intel Macs and Windows PCs are not supported.
- **Still photography:** Native RAW still photo support is currently listed by Colourlab as forthcoming. Photographers who shoot hybrid video projects will benefit most, while stills-only photographers will find it overpowered and cumbersome for catalog workflows.
- **Pricing:** $49/month, $399/year annual subscription, or $799 perpetual lifetime license, backed by a 14-day free trial.

### 3D LUT Creator (Precision grid color grading and 3D LUT export)

![Promotional image of a laptop displaying the 3D LUT Creator logo (Source: 3D LUT Creator official website).](/assets/3d-lut-creator.webp)

If your goal with Fylm.ai is creating custom 3D LUTs with surgical color accuracy, [3D LUT Creator](https://3dlutcreator.com/) is a dedicated standalone color-manipulation application developed by Oleg Sharonov for macOS and Windows, featuring a direct two-way bridge to Adobe Photoshop.

Unlike traditional grading wheels that adjust broad tonal ranges, 3D LUT Creator uses a 2D A/B color grid and C/L contrast grid. You can select any precise hue and saturation point (for instance, shifting skin tones away from magenta or twisting green foliage into warm amber) and drag the grid vertex directly without shifting neighboring colors or introducing digital banding.

**Capabilities and export workflow:**
- **Native export formats:** Saves looks directly as industry-standard 3D LUT files in **.cube, .3dl, and .csv** formats.
- **Lightroom and Capture One workflow:** 3D LUT Creator does not natively export Camera Raw `.xmp` profiles or Capture One `.icc` camera profiles directly in-app. Instead, photographers apply LUTs via Adobe Photoshop adjustment layers or convert them to enhanced profiles using external conversion utilities.
- **Photoshop integration:** Send images and LUTs back and forth between Photoshop and 3D LUT Creator with a single click.
- **Perpetual pricing tiers:** Sold under a lifetime license with free updates through all version 1.xx releases:
  - **Grading Edition ($99):** Includes 2 core tabs (A/B color grid and Curves), Photoshop two-way integration, Color Match reference transfer, and a maximum LUT size of 33 (33×33×33). Other editing tabs (Channels, Volume, C/L grid, 2D Curves, and Masks) are not included.
  - **Standard Edition ($199):** Adds C/L contrast grid, Channels, Volume, 2D Curves, and Masks. Maximum LUT size remains 33 (33×33×33). Note that the Color Match tool is disabled in Standard.
  - **Professional Edition ($249):** Unlocks a maximum LUT size of 96 (96×96×96) for high-fidelity transitions, restores the Color Match tool, adds color target matching (such as ColorChecker charts), Log and RAW profile support, a waveform analyzer, and batch processing.

### Pixieset Photo Editor (Formerly Polarr Next: AI batch editing)

For photographers who previously looked to Polarr for batch reference matching, it is important to understand the product evolution. Polarr's professional AI batch editing tool (formerly known as Polarr Next) transitioned into [Pixieset Photo Editor](https://pixieset.com/photo-editor/).

Pixieset Photo Editor is a dedicated desktop and browser-based platform designed specifically for high-volume portrait and wedding photographers. It uses artificial intelligence to analyze reference styles and apply consistent exposure, white balance, and tone adjustments across entire RAW catalogs.

**Pricing and access:**
- **Free tier ($0):** Basic editing and evaluation.
- **Paid plan:** **$12/month billed annually** ($144/year) or **$18/month on monthly billing**, unlocking full AI editing, custom AI styles, RAW photo support, and unlimited exports.

*(Note: The standalone [Polarr app](https://www.polarr.com/) remains available on iOS, Android, macOS, and Windows as a consumer image editor with creative filters and selective masking for $3.99/month or $19.99/year, but is not designed for catalog-scale professional RAW batch grading).*

### Media.io (Free online auto-color correction)

[Media.io Auto Color Correct](https://www.media.io/auto-color-correct.html) is a lightweight, web-based utility for photographers and casual content creators who just need a quick, automated image fix.

Unlike Fylm.ai, Media.io is not a creative color grading or LUT creation suite. It uses automated algorithms to balance underexposed shadows, remove color casts, and correct white balance in a single click. It is completely browser-based, requires no installation, and is free for occasional use, though it lacks RAW file support and professional profile exports.

### Lightroom Classic and Capture One Pro (The tools you already own)

Before investing in a third-party color grading suite, it is worth evaluating the tools already inside your primary RAW converter:

- **Adobe Lightroom Classic:** Features 3-Way Color Grading wheels (Shadows, Midtones, Highlights, and Global), Point Color for surgical hue-saturation-luminance sampling, the Calibration panel (a favorite among portrait photographers for rich skin tones), and tone curves.
- **Capture One Pro:** Includes an industry-standard Advanced Color Editor with dedicated Skin Tone uniformity sliders, 3-Way Color Balance wheels, and per-layer opacity grading.

If your objective is simply clean, consistent, flattering color across weddings or portraits, mastering your RAW editor's built-in toolset often eliminates the need for external grading software.

## FAQ

**What is Fylm.ai used for?**
Fylm.ai is a cloud-based colour grading platform that uses ACEScct colour management to build cinematic looks and 3D LUTs. Photographers use it because it can export enhanced XMP camera profiles for Adobe Lightroom and Camera Raw, as well as ICC profiles for Capture One Pro.

**How much does Fylm.ai cost?**
Fylm.ai offers four plans (billed annually): a Free tier (3 projects, 8-bit web grading), Lite ($7/month billed annually / $84/year, with unlimited local storage and AI tools), Pro ($15/month billed annually / $180/year for 1 user, 10 GB cloud storage, Premiere extension, and collaboration), and Team ($30/month billed annually / $360/year including 3 user seats, 25 GB cloud storage, and priority support). Additional user seats on Pro and Team cost $15/user/month.

**What is the closest alternative to Fylm.ai for photographers?**
For film emulation and profiles inside your editing software, Dehancer Photo is the closest alternative. For precision color grading and 3D LUT creation with one-time perpetual licensing, 3D LUT Creator is the most versatile choice. For AI batch styling, Pixieset Photo Editor (successor to Polarr Next) provides automated catalog editing.

**What happened to Color.io?**
Color.io announced the shutdown of its online platform on 31 December 2025. While existing Pro subscribers received an offline legacy build, the service is discontinued and closed to new users.

**Does Colourlab Ai 4 work inside Lightroom or Photoshop?**
No. Colourlab Ai 4 is a standalone macOS application that requires Apple silicon (M-series) and macOS 14+. It connects directly with DaVinci Resolve Studio via API, and with Premiere Pro and Final Cut Pro via OTIO/XML. Native RAW still photo support is currently listed as forthcoming.

**What is the difference between Polarr and Pixieset Photo Editor?**
Polarr Next transitioned into Pixieset Photo Editor, which is a professional AI batch editing tool for portrait and wedding photographers ($12/month billed annually or $18/month monthly). The standard Polarr app is a consumer mobile and desktop filter editor ($3.99/month or $19.99/year) that is not built for catalog-scale RAW grading.

**Do photographers actually need a dedicated colour grading tool?**
Not always. If you need authentic film emulsion physics (like grain, halation, and bloom), multi-camera video matching, or custom 3D LUT authoring, dedicated tools are invaluable. If your goal is clean, consistent portrait or event colour, your RAW editor's built-in tools are usually more than sufficient.
