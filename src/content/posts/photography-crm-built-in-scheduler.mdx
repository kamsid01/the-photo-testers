## Why a built-in scheduler matters

Plenty of photographers run booking through a separate tool. Calendly for the call, a CRM for everything else.

That works, until it doesn't.

The problem is the seams. When your scheduler lives outside your CRM, a booking doesn't become a project. The client's details don't flow into your contract. You copy information from one place to another, and every copy is a chance to get a date wrong.

For a wedding photographer juggling twenty couples at once, those seams are where things fall through.

A built-in scheduler closes the gap. Someone books a consultation, and it lands in the same system that holds their contract, their invoice, and their gallery. One place. No re-typing.

That's the whole pitch. For a lot of photographers, it's worth choosing a CRM around.

We signed up for the main photography CRMs, set up real scheduling links, and booked test sessions through each one. Here's how they stack up.

## FAQ

**Does Studio Ninja have a built-in scheduler?**
No. Studio Ninja has no native scheduler. You connect an external tool to handle bookings.

**Which photography CRM has the best built-in scheduler?**
In our testing, HoneyBook's was the most capable, because the booking flows directly into contracts and invoices instead of sitting off to the side.

**Is there a free photography CRM with a scheduler?**
Pixieset's free tier includes scheduling, making it the cheapest way to get a built-in scheduler when you're starting out.

**Can I just use Calendly with my CRM instead?**
Yes, and many photographers do. The tradeoff is the manual step between a booking and the rest of your workflow. A built-in scheduler removes that step.
