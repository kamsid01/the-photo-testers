# Pic-Time vs Pixieset vs ShootProof (2026 Comparison)

By The Photo Testers · Updated August 2026 · 9 min read

*We research every tool and keep pricing current. Some links below are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.*

Choosing a client gallery feels like marrying into a family. You don’t just get a nice photo grid - you inherit their print labs, their email marketing quirks, and their billing structure.

In 2026, **Pic-Time**, **Pixieset**, and **ShootProof** dominate the space. But they aren't interchangeable. One is a sales powerhouse disguised as a gallery. One is a gorgeous all-in-one suite. One is a pragmatic workhorse.

We’ve tested all three extensively to see where they stand right now. Here is the honest breakdown of which one you should choose based on your actual business model.

---

## The 10-Second Summary

If you hate reading and just want the answer:

*   **Choose Pic-Time if:** You want to maximize print sales passively. Their automated marketing campaigns are lightyears ahead of the competition.
*   **Choose Pixieset if:** You value aesthetics and want an all-in-one ecosystem (website, CRM, galleries) that is effortless to use.
*   **Choose ShootProof if:** You do high-volume work (like schools or sports), need robust client invoicing, or want to avoid commission fees on lower-tier plans.

---

## 1. Design & Client Experience

Your clients don't care about the backend. They only see what you deliver.

### Pixieset: The Apple of Galleries
Pixieset is undeniably pretty. It’s clean, modern, and very hard to make look bad. Their recent layout updates in 2025 gave photographers more control over the grid and typography, but it still maintains that signature minimalist vibe. The client experience is frictionless - scrolling, favoriting, and downloading are intuitive even for less tech-savvy grandparents.

### Pic-Time: The Editorial Magazine
Pic-Time feels high-end. Their galleries look like editorial spreads. The transition animations are smooth, and the way they integrate the store into the gallery viewing experience is seamless. Clients don't have to leave the gallery to see what a canvas would look like on their wall - it's mocked up right there.

### ShootProof: The Functional Choice
ShootProof has improved their designs significantly over the last few years, but it still feels slightly more utilitarian compared to the other two. It’s clean and professional, but it lacks the "wow" factor of Pic-Time or Pixieset. However, its straightforward design is excellent for high-volume proofing where clarity is more important than vibes.

**Winner for Design:** **Pixieset** (for pure ease and minimalism) or **Pic-Time** (for a high-end, editorial feel).

---

## 2. The Store & Print Sales

This is the biggest differentiator. If you want to make money *after* the shoot, pay attention here.

### Pic-Time: The Unfair Advantage
Pic-Time isn’t just a gallery; it’s an automated sales machine. You can set up campaigns (e.g., "Abandoned Cart," "Anniversary Sale," "Holiday Early Bird") and Pic-Time will automatically email your clients, generate banners inside their gallery, and apply countdown timers. It is staggeringly effective. Many photographers report their print sales doubling simply by switching to Pic-Time and turning on the automations.

### Pixieset: Beautiful but Basic
Pixieset’s store is gorgeous. Setting up price sheets is incredibly easy, and the checkout process is smooth. However, their marketing tools are basic. You can send coupons, but you have to do it manually. There are no automated email campaigns or dynamic gallery banners pushing a sale.

### ShootProof: Flexible but Clunky
ShootProof allows for great flexibility with price sheets, especially for complex volume photography (e.g., packages, different price tiers for different galleries). However, the storefront itself feels a bit dated compared to the others, and the marketing automation is virtually non-existent.

**Winner for Print Sales:** **Pic-Time**, by a landslide.

## 3. Storage and Pricing (2026 Reality Check)

Let’s look at the actual costs. (Note: These are based on annual billing as of mid-2026).

### Pixieset Pricing
Pixieset limits your storage on lower plans, but they *do not* charge a commission on your print sales if you are on a paid plan.
*   **Free:** 3GB storage (15% commission)
*   **Basic ($14/mo):** 10GB storage (0% commission)
*   **Plus ($24/mo):** 100GB storage (0% commission)
*   **Pro ($34/mo):** 1,000GB storage (0% commission)
*   **Ultimate ($44/mo):** Unlimited storage (0% commission)
*   *(Note: Pixieset also offers "Suite" pricing which includes their CRM and Website builder).*

### Pic-Time Pricing
Pic-Time gives you more storage early on, but you have to pay a higher monthly fee to escape the 15% commission on print sales.
*   **Free:** 20GB storage (15% commission)
*   **Professional ($20/mo):** 100GB storage (15% commission)
*   **Advanced ($34/mo):** Unlimited storage (0% commission)
*   *(Note: The $34/mo plan is almost mandatory if you plan on selling prints).*

### ShootProof Pricing
ShootProof prices based on the *number of photos* you upload, not the gigabyte size. This is great if you shoot smaller JPEGs, but terrible if you upload massive files.
*   **Free:** 100 Photos (15% commission)
*   **1,500 Photos ($12/mo):** (0% commission)
*   **5,000 Photos ($24/mo):** (0% commission)
*   **25,000 Photos ($40/mo):** (0% commission)
*   **Unlimited ($60/mo):** (0% commission)

**Winner for Pricing:** It’s a tie based on your needs. **Pixieset** is best for low-volume shooters who don't want to pay commissions. **Pic-Time** is best if you want unlimited storage and high sales.

---

## 4. The Ecosystem (What else do they do?)

Galleries rarely exist in a vacuum anymore.

### Pixieset: The All-in-One King
Pixieset wants to run your entire business. Their "Suite" includes a Website Builder (which is genuinely excellent), a CRM (Studio Manager) for contracts and invoicing, and the Galleries. If you want one login and one bill for your whole business, Pixieset is unbeatable.

### ShootProof: Contracts and Invoicing
ShootProof has built-in invoicing and contracts natively. It’s not a full CRM, but it handles the essential business paperwork very well, especially for standard portrait and wedding workflows.

### Pic-Time: Slideshows and Blogs
Pic-Time includes a phenomenal slideshow builder (with licensed music) built right into the gallery. They also recently introduced a blogging tool that pulls images directly from your galleries to create SEO-friendly blog posts. However, they do not have a built-in CRM for contracts or invoicing.

---

## The Final Verdict

Here is exactly how you should choose in 2026:

**1. Go with Pixieset if:**
You want an all-in-one solution. If you want your website, contracts, invoices, and galleries all under one roof, and you value a minimalist aesthetic above all else. It is the easiest platform to use.

**2. Go with Pic-Time if:**
You are a wedding or portrait photographer who wants to increase revenue. The automated marketing campaigns will literally pay for the software subscription within the first few months. The editorial look and built-in slideshows are a massive bonus.

**3. Go with ShootProof if:**
You are a volume photographer (schools, sports, dance studios). The way ShootProof handles passwords, packages, and high-volume downloads is still superior for that specific workflow.
