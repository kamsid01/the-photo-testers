# Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: June 2026

The Photo Testers is supported by its readers. Here is exactly how that works.

## We use affiliate links

Some of the links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and sign up for or buy a product, we may earn a commission. This comes at no extra cost to you, you pay the same price you would have paid anyway.

We have affiliate relationships with some of the software companies we review, which may include HoneyBook, Dubsado, Pixieset, Studio Ninja, and others.

## It does not change our recommendations

This is the part that matters. Earning a commission never changes what we write or which tool we recommend. We recommend the tool we genuinely think is best for the reader described, and we have recommended free tools and cheaper tools over ones that pay us more.

Our reasoning is simple and a little self-interested: a review that pushes the wrong tool to make a quick commission is a review nobody trusts twice. This site only works if you can rely on it. So the honest recommendation is the only one we make.

## What we do not do

We do not let affiliate relationships decide our verdicts. We do not hide the cons of tools that pay us. We do not recommend tools we have not tested.

## Questions

If you ever want to know whether a specific link is an affiliate link, or have any questions about how we are funded, [contact us](/contact) and we will tell you straight.

This disclosure is provided in line with FTC guidelines on affiliate marketing.
