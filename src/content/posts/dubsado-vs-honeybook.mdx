## What they have in common

Before the differences, know that these are both strong, full-featured CRMs. Both handle the core wedding workflow: leads, proposals, contracts, invoices, payments, scheduling, and client communication. Both have built-in schedulers. Both are priced similarly at the entry level.

You won't go wrong with either in terms of capability. The real question is how you like to work.

## Ease of use: HoneyBook wins

This is the clearest difference. HoneyBook is easier to set up and start using. The templates are ready to go, the interface is intuitive, and you can have a working booking flow running quickly.

Dubsado asks more up front. It's built to be configured to your exact process, which means an afternoon of setup before it feels right. Powerful, but not plug-and-play.

If you want to be up and running fast, HoneyBook is the smoother road. If you don't mind investing setup time for a tailored system, Dubsado pays it back.

## Client experience: HoneyBook edges it

For wedding work, the experience your couples have when booking matters, it's part of why they choose you. HoneyBook's client-facing pages, proposals, and payment flows feel polished and professional out of the box.

Dubsado's client experience is good and fully customizable, but getting it as polished as HoneyBook's default takes more work. Out of the box, HoneyBook looks more refined to your couples.

## Customization: Dubsado wins

This is where Dubsado pulls ahead. If you want to build a precise inquiry-to-delivery pipeline, with custom forms, conditional workflows, and detailed automation, Dubsado gives you more control.

HoneyBook is customizable too, but within more defined limits. For photographers who have a specific process they want the tool to match exactly, Dubsado is the more flexible choice.

## Pricing: close, with an edge to Dubsado annually

At the entry level both start around $20/mo. The difference shows up annually, where Dubsado often works out cheaper. Dubsado also offers a free trial with no time limit (capped to a few clients), so you can test it thoroughly before paying.

HoneyBook's trial is time-limited but lets you experience the full flow. Pricing changes, so check current plans, but on cost, Dubsado tends to have a slight edge for annual payers.

## So, which one?

**Choose HoneyBook if** you want a CRM that works smoothly out of the box, you value a polished client experience, and you'd rather not spend a day configuring software.

**Choose Dubsado if** you want deep control over your workflow, you're happy to invest setup time, and you want a slightly lower annual cost.

For the typical wedding photographer who wants to book couples and get back to shooting, HoneyBook is the easier, smoother pick. For the photographer who wants their tool shaped exactly to their process, Dubsado rewards the effort.

The good news: both let you trial before committing. Try each with a real inquiry and see which fits how you work.
