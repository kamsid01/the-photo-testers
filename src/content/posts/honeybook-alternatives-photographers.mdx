## Why people leave HoneyBook

HoneyBook is a good tool. But it's not the right fit for everyone, and a few things send photographers looking:

**Price.** HoneyBook's plans have crept up, and for a solo photographer the cost can feel steep next to leaner tools.

**More than they need.** HoneyBook does a lot. If you only want booking, contracts, and invoices, you may be paying for breadth you'll never touch.

**They want something photography-specific.** HoneyBook serves all kinds of freelancers. Some photographers prefer a tool built only for photography work.

Whatever your reason, here are the alternatives worth testing, and what each one is actually good for.

## 1. Dubsado: best overall alternative

If you want the closest thing to HoneyBook's all-in-one experience, Dubsado is it. Contracts, invoices, scheduling, workflows, it covers the same ground.

## 2. Studio Ninja: cheapest serious option

Studio Ninja is built specifically for photographers, and it's simpler and cheaper than HoneyBook. If you want a focused tool without the broader freelancer features, this is a strong pick.

It does the core jobs well: leads, bookings, contracts, invoices. It doesn't have a native scheduler (you connect an external tool), so factor that in if scheduling matters to you.

## 3. Pixieset Studio: best free option

Pixieset is best known for galleries, but its Studio suite includes CRM features, contracts, invoices, scheduling, on a free tier. For a photographer just starting out, it's the cheapest way to get organized.

The free tier has limits, and the CRM side is less deep than Dubsado or HoneyBook. But if you already use Pixieset for galleries, having booking and invoicing in the same place is a real convenience.

## So, which alternative?

**Want the closest thing to HoneyBook?** Dubsado. It covers the same ground and often costs less annually.

**On a tight budget?** Studio Ninja if you want a paid photographer-specific tool, or Pixieset's free tier if you're just starting.

The honest move is to trial two or three. Most offer a free trial or free tier, so you can feel the difference before you commit.
