## How Pic-Time fits into your photography stack

Pic-Time is a client gallery delivery and automated print-sales platform, not a CRM. It delivers finished high-resolution photos, hosts client stores, and automates marketing campaigns. It does not handle initial inquiries, client booking contracts, questionnaires, or shoot invoicing.

When photographers search for "a CRM that integrates with Pic-Time", they need a workflow where lead capture, contracts, scheduling, and retainers happen in their CRM, with an automated or seamless handoff to Pic-Time for delivery and print sales once the shoot is completed.

Contrary to common misconceptions, native integrations do exist. HoneyBook and Studio Ninja provide direct API connections, while Dubsado connects via Zapier automations. Here is how the leading options compare.

## 1. HoneyBook + Pic-Time: best overall for North American photographers

HoneyBook handles the complete front-end client lifecycle: lead inquiries, smart proposals, legally binding contracts, payment processing, and automated client communications. Pic-Time handles high-res delivery and automated store marketing after the shoot.

The integration is native and configured directly in HoneyBook under **Company Settings > Integrations > Pic-Time**. Once authorized, you can enable automatic gallery creation whenever a client makes their first payment on an invoice, or manually link and launch a Pic-Time project directly from any HoneyBook project workspace. Client names, emails, and event dates pass across automatically.

## 2. Studio Ninja + Pic-Time: best for international photographers and order tracking

Studio Ninja was built specifically for photographers and offers one of the tightest two-way Pic-Time integrations available. Configured in Studio Ninja **Settings > Integrations > Pic-Time**, it links your accounts with your Pic-Time login credentials.

From any Studio Ninja Job profile, you can create a brand-new Pic-Time gallery or link an existing gallery with a single click. Even better, Studio Ninja automatically imports client print orders and store revenue from Pic-Time directly into the Job’s financial ledger, logging product sales and payments without manual entry. Full multi-currency support (GBP, EUR, AUD, USD) makes it ideal for UK, European, and Australian businesses.

## 3. Dubsado + Pic-Time: most customizable via Zapier

Dubsado is popular among photographers who need deep questionnaire logic, custom client portal design, and intricate multi-step workflow automations. Unlike HoneyBook and Studio Ninja, Dubsado does not currently have a native direct integration with Pic-Time.

Instead, the connection is achieved via Zapier or direct client portal embedding. With Zapier, you can set a trigger such as "Contract Signed" or "Payment Received" in Dubsado to automatically create a new project in Pic-Time. Alternatively, photographers include the unique Pic-Time gallery link directly in automated delivery emails or the Dubsado client portal.

## The bottom line: which setup fits your business?

If you want an official native integration with automatic gallery creation upon deposit payment, HoneyBook is the smoothest option for US and Canadian photographers. If you want direct print-order syncing into your financial records or operate in the UK, Europe, or Australia, Studio Ninja is the standout choice. If you require complex custom questionnaires and multi-part contracts, Dubsado paired with Zapier provides complete control.

In all setups, Pic-Time remains your dedicated presentation and print-monetization layer, ensuring your clients receive a premium delivery experience while your CRM handles legal, financial, and administrative tasks.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does HoneyBook integrate with Pic-Time?**
Yes. HoneyBook features an official direct native integration with Pic-Time. Configured in Settings > Integrations, HoneyBook can automatically generate a Pic-Time gallery whenever a client completes their initial payment, or photographers can manually link galleries from the project workspace.

**Does Studio Ninja integrate with Pic-Time?**
Yes. Studio Ninja provides a direct native integration that lets photographers create or link Pic-Time galleries directly from job cards. It also automatically imports client print orders and store revenue from Pic-Time into Studio Ninja’s job invoices and financial reports.

**Does Dubsado integrate with Pic-Time?**
Dubsado does not currently have a native direct integration with Pic-Time. However, you can connect them seamlessly via Zapier (triggering project creation upon contract signing or payment) or by adding Pic-Time gallery links directly to client email workflows and client portals.

**Is Pic-Time a CRM?**
No. Pic-Time is a client gallery delivery and automated print-sales platform. It does not provide client contracts, booking proposals, or shoot invoicing, which is why pairing it with a dedicated CRM like HoneyBook or Studio Ninja creates a complete business workflow.
