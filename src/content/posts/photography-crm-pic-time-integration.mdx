## How Pic-Time fits into your stack

First, a quick clarification, because this trips people up. Pic-Time is a gallery delivery and print-sales platform, not a CRM. It's where you deliver photos and sell prints and albums. It doesn't do booking, contracts, or client invoicing for your shoots.

So "a CRM that integrates with Pic-Time" really means: which CRM works well alongside Pic-Time, so you have one tool for the client workflow and Pic-Time for delivery and sales.

The honest reality is there's no deep, official one-click integration between the major CRMs and Pic-Time. What you can do is run a CRM for the front end and Pic-Time for the back end, and connect them with automation where it helps.
