# Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains what information The Photo Testers collects and how we use it. We keep this short and plain on purpose.

## What we collect

**Email address:** only if you choose to subscribe to our newsletter. We use it to send you new comparisons and pricing updates. Nothing else. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

**Analytics data:** we use analytics to understand how people use the site, which pages are read, roughly where visitors come from, what device they use. This is aggregated and helps us decide what to write next.

**Cookies:** our site and our analytics and advertising partners may use cookies, small files stored in your browser. You can disable cookies in your browser settings if you prefer.

## How we use it

We use this information to run the newsletter, improve the site, and understand what content is useful. We do not sell your personal data.

## Third parties

Some third-party services we use may collect data under their own policies, including:
- Google Analytics: site usage
- Affiliate networks: when you click an affiliate link, the destination site may track the referral

## Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time, disable cookies in your browser, and request that we delete any personal information we hold about you by contacting us.

## Contact

Questions about your privacy or this policy? Email us at hello@thephototesters.com or via our [Contact](/contact) page.
