# Contact

We read everything that comes in.

**Email:** hello@thephototesters.com

Here is what we would genuinely love to hear about:

**A tool we should test.** If there is a piece of photography software you wish we covered, tell us. We are always adding to the list.

**A correction.** If a price is out of date, a feature has changed, or we got something wrong, let us know and we will fix it. Accurate is the whole point.

**A question.** Trying to choose between two tools and not sure which fits your situation? Send the details and we will give you an honest steer.

We are a small operation, so it might take a few days to reply, but a real person reads every message.
