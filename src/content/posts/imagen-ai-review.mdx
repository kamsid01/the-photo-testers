## What Imagen AI is

Imagen AI is AI editing software that learns your personal editing style and applies it automatically across large batches of photos inside Lightroom Classic. Instead of hand-editing an entire gallery, you run Smart Previews through Imagen and it applies your look, white balance, exposure, contrast, color, and tone in minutes.

Its reputation is built on quality and consistency. Photographers who use it routinely describe being impressed at how closely it matches their own editing decisions, and independent reviewers report it has captured a significant share of the professional batch-editing market. It also added AI culling more recently, though editing is unmistakably its core.

## Pricing: Pay-as-you-go vs Limitless

Imagen offers two primary pricing paths for photographers: a usage-based Pay-As-You-Go model and a flat-rate Limitless subscription.

- **Pay-as-you-go (PAYG):** Starts at $0.05 per edited photo, subject to a $7/month minimum spend. Suitable for lower-volume portrait photographers or seasonal shooters who pause during winter.
- **Limitless plan:** A flat annual subscription (approx. $540–$720/year billed annually, or roughly $45–$60/month equivalent) covering unlimited AI editing with a personal AI profile and AI tools included. *Note: Limitless is strictly for eligible individual solo photographers editing their own work.*
- **Optional AI tools:** On PAYG, crop ($0.01), straighten ($0.01), subject mask ($0.01), and smooth skin ($0.01) cost extra per photo. Enabling all four brings editing to $0.09 per photo.
- **Business plans:** Custom pooled volume tiers for multi-shooter studios, associate teams, and high-volume post-production studios.

**Understanding the volume math:** Pricing applies to selected, edited frames—not raw capture. A wedding culled to 700 delivered photos costs $35 on PAYG base, or $49 with crop and straighten ($0.07/photo). For a solo wedding shooter editing 14,000 photos across 20 weddings, Limitless provides predictable flat annual billing ($540–$720/year). For multi-shooter studios delivering 35,000+ photos, PAYG costs over $1,750/year base, where local flat-fee rivals like Aftershoot ($240–$480/year) offer substantial cost savings.

**Official pricing documentation:** [Imagen Pricing & Plans Support Guide](https://support.imagen-ai.com/hc/en-us/articles/4544978323857-Imagen-pricing-and-plans). Imagen provides 1,000 free AI editing credits upon signup so you can test real catalogs on your own computer.

## Editing: the reason to buy

Editing is where Imagen shines, and reviewers broadly rate it the strongest here. It builds a **Personal AI Profile** by analyzing your previously edited Lightroom catalogs, ideally 3,000 to 5,000 images or more, and the more consistent your back catalog, the more accurately it matches your style. Users consistently report results close to their own edits, especially for color correction, color grading, exposure, and tone.

The most-cited strength is skin tones. Reviewers repeatedly single out Imagen for handling skin tones on weddings and portraits, exactly where weaker AI editing tends to fall apart. It also applies skin smoothing and local adjustments (via masking) across a whole gallery at once, a real time-saver for portrait work.

For newer photographers without an extensive edited catalog, **Talent Profiles** are the ideal solution: pre-built models trained on established photographers. Instead of applying one flat preset, the AI analyzes each frame and makes nuanced exposure, color, and contrast adjustments. It offers an exceptionally fast way to get consistent edits back in Lightroom Classic.

## The honest limitations

This is where a real review earns its keep.

**It's not a finish-line tool.** Even enthusiastic users are clear: you don't run photos through Imagen and send them straight to clients. Most still do a polishing pass, masking, subject removal, tweaks on hero images. Reviewers describe it getting you to roughly 90–95 percent, with your artistry finishing the rest.

**The culling is weak at low volume.** This is the most useful caveat in independent reviews: photographers who shoot conservatively (under about 2,000 frames per event) report Imagen's culling suggestions unreliable enough to skip. If culling is your real pain point, a dedicated culler like Aftershoot or Narrative Select handles it with greater control.

**It's cloud-only and Lightroom Classic only.** Imagen sends Smart Previews to its cloud servers for processing, so you need a stable internet connection and cannot work offline. And it's built around Lightroom Classic; if you use Capture One or another editor, confirm compatibility before subscribing.

## FAQ

**How much does Imagen AI cost?**
Imagen offers two main pricing options: Pay-as-you-go starts at $0.05 per photo (plus $0.01 for optional tools like crop, straighten, subject mask, and smooth skin, up to $0.09/photo) with a $7/month minimum spend. For eligible solo photographers shooting their own work, the flat Limitless plan costs approximately $540–$720/year ($45–$60/month equivalent) for unlimited editing with AI tools included. Studios with associate teams use custom Business tiers.

**Is Imagen AI better than Aftershoot?**
For pure editing quality, reviewers generally say yes, Imagen is the stronger editor, especially for skin tones. But Aftershoot is better value at studio volume (flat workstation pricing), works completely offline, and includes deeper culling and retouching tools. Many photographers use both. See our full [Aftershoot vs Imagen comparison](/aftershoot-vs-imagen).

**Does Imagen AI work offline?**
No. It processes in the cloud, sending Smart Previews to its servers, so you need a stable internet connection. If offline work matters, Aftershoot processes locally instead.

**Can Imagen AI edit exactly like me?**
Reviewers say it gets close once trained on a large, consistent catalog of your past edits (ideally 3,000 to 5,000+ images). It handles color, exposure, and tone in your style well, but most photographers still do a light polishing pass on final hero frames.

**Is Imagen AI good for culling?**
It's a secondary feature compared to dedicated tools. Photographers looking for fast, nuanced selection typically prefer dedicated culling applications like Aftershoot or Narrative Select.
